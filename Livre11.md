# UNDERSTANDING LINKEDIN {.heading_s3H}

LinkedIn is a social media platform with a strong emphasis on business
networking and career advancement.

By sharing updates and engaging with other users, you may use LinkedIn
to promote your professional brand, look for opportunities, and display
your résumé.

Even though LinkedIn is free, a paid version called LinkedIn Premium
gives extra services including online workshops and seminars as well as
details on who is looking at and searching for your profile.

LinkedIn is a social network, just like Facebook, Instagram, Twitter,
and a dozen other platforms. Additionally, Microsoft owns it, just like
other major tech firms.

But unlike most social networks, LinkedIn is a professional networking
platform to facilitate business contacts, resume sharing, and job
search.

# The basics for LinkedIn {#part0005.xhtml#aK6 .heading_s3T}

LinkedIn is quite similar to social networking sites like Facebook yet
having a professional focus. It is founded on ideas such as connecting
with friends (or, in the case of LinkedIn, \"connections\"), making
updates, sharing and liking information, and IMing other users.

LinkedIn also gives concepts you already know from Facebook a
professional spin. Your profile, for instance, transforms into a résumé
with your professional history, achievements, letters of recommendation,
and colleagues\' suggestions.

Anyone on LinkedIn can read your profile, which is comparable to a
resume.

The website also offers unique features, such as a fully functional
career board where you can look for jobs and submit applications.

Now that so many employment services are LinkedIn-compatible, you might
be able to share your LinkedIn profile when looking for a job, saving
you the trouble of meticulously filling out a lengthy online form with
your work history.

The news stream on Facebook can be compared to a brief tour of LinkedIn
Home. You may view posts from other LinkedIn users here, as well as post
your content for others to view, like, share, and comment on. Similar to
Facebook, you may choose to share your ideas with everyone or simply
your network\'s contacts.

Anyone who uses a social networking platform like Facebook will be
familiar with the look and function of LinkedIn\'s Home page. The
site\'s important pages are displayed at the top of this image.

You may view your current network of contacts in My Network, where you
can also look for new LinkedIn members to add to your network. You may
also view the invitations you already have from other people.

Jobs is a thorough career website. You can do a job search, apply, learn
more about businesses that interest you, and much more. When new job ads
are made, you can set up email notifications.

Jobs can be found using several search criteria, including area and
keyword. Additionally, you can gain unique information,
[]{#part0005.xhtml#page_9}such as whether you know somebody who now
works for the organization you\'re considering.

Messaging connects you to an instant messaging service where you can
speak with contacts in your network. If you pay for certain levels of
LinkedIn Premium, you may also use it to send messages to people who are
not in your network using a feature called InMail.

You can locate your profile, which is like your resume, on me. You can
display your employment history, accomplishments, education, present
location, and more here to demonstrate your professional development. If
you have a LinkedIn profile, it should be just as polished as your
actual CV because recruiters and business connections may look at it.

# OPPORTUNITIES ON LINKEDIN {.heading_s4M}

The largest business-focused networking platform targeted particularly
at professionals is called LinkedIn. It has members in more than 200
countries and more than 500 million. You may establish an online
professional identity with the aid of a well-written LinkedIn profile,
which can help you gain access to possibilities and networks that you
might not have known about without the aid of social media.

When recruiters and employers use LinkedIn to search for candidates, you
can use it to showcase your profile, expertise, recommendations, and
connections. Your profile not only gives recruiters and employers their
first professional impression of you, but it also establishes your
credibility in your field and draws attention to your accomplishments.
Many people continue to undervalue the role that LinkedIn can play in
their job search and are occasionally hesitant to use social media. This
chapter outlines seven benefits of using LinkedIn in your job search.

1\. Getting in touch with recruiters and hiring managers

LinkedIn is used by 93% of recruiters for candidate research and
recruitment. You can have an online personal brand on LinkedIn to
attract the attention of recruiters and influential decision-makers.
This implies that you have an online personal brand that shows up in
search results when your name is typed into a search engine like Google.
For this reason, you must guarantee that your LinkedIn page is treated
similarly to your resume and that the information on it effectively
markets you.

2\. Showcasing your leadership skills, knowledge, and credibility.

Employers and recruiters are more likely to trust you if you have a
LinkedIn profile since they can see your connections, recommendations,
and other proof of your value-added contributions.

3\. Tool for research

You may investigate businesses, interviewers, recruiters, and hiring
managers on LinkedIn if you have an account there. This is useful before
submitting applications, finding out who will be interviewing you, and
conducting the interview itself.

4\. The employment board on LinkedIn is excellent.

The LinkedIn website offers new opportunities that might not be
available on conventional job forums. Using LinkedIn Apply, you may
directly apply for jobs, save job searches, and indicate to recruiters
that you are interested in learning about opportunities.

5\. You can get validation from others for your abilities and
capabilities.

Allow others to congratulate you for your abilities and prior work.
Anyone who views your profile will be able to see any recommendations or
endorsements from others, which will demonstrate that you have the
support of other experts for the talents you claim to possess. This has
great connection-making power. Through the recommendations and
testimonials that LinkedIn lets your connections post on your profile,
you can increase your trustworthiness.

6\. Learning tool for Businesses

LinkedIn is an excellent resource for learning about companies and the
individuals who work there. Using this, you can specifically target
members of your network who work for companies that you want to work
for.

7\. Joining groups on LinkedIn

Groups are an excellent method to network with professionals in your
sector as well as other recent grads and others with similar
professional interests. You can participate in discussions in groups.
This is a fantastic method to demonstrate your expertise on topics
relevant to your sector, pose inquiries, and strike up dialogues with
important figures in important organizations.

There is virtually no disadvantage to having a LinkedIn profile, only
advantages. Participate in forums, join organizations, and connect with
others who share your interests. Most importantly, LinkedIn is entirely
free. Job hunting is a difficult process, and applying for jobs online
every day is a job in and of itself!

From the standpoint of a job seeker, LinkedIn is an additional resource
you may utilize to help advance your job hunt. To make sure you are
efficiently sourcing prospects, it is crucial to optimize your profile
and proactively use LinkedIn. Use LinkedIn groups and corporate sites to
browse group member profiles, make new contacts, learn about relevant
news, and contribute your knowledge to discussions.

# HOW TO EFFECTIVELY USE LINKEDIN {.heading_s4M}

The largest professional networking site in the world is LinkedIn. With
756 million users, it\'s also one of the most significant social media
platforms. There are a lot of possible contacts there!

Members utilize the website to communicate with clients, coworkers, and
business partners. However, it has a lot more potential. For instance,
it may raise your profile, increase brand recognition, and aid in hiring
the best candidates.

This chapter examines nine strategies for making the most of your
LinkedIn profile for business, professional, and organizational success.

It should be noted that a basic LinkedIn account is free. Premium
accounts provide additional features based on your needs. Included in
them are Premium Business (\$59.99 per month) and Premium Career
(\$29.99 per month), both of which are geared toward job hunters. The
Sales Navigator and Recruiter packages come with a higher price tag.

Nine Tips for Using LinkedIn to Your Advantage

1\. Complete Your LinkedIn Profile

A strong component of your brand may be your profile. Everyone who has
signed up for LinkedIn will be able to see it (unless you set it to
private mode). You can also have a public profile that can be seen by
outside search engines, allowing LinkedIn users who are not signed in to
see it.

Only \"full\" profiles are found by LinkedIn\'s proprietary search
algorithm, and these can receive more than 20 times as many views as
incomplete profiles. Therefore, you must finish yours. The following are
the key points to keep in mind:

Include a decent photo of yourself, preferably one that was taken by a
pro. Look intelligent, smile, and avoid any obtrusive objects in the
photograph.

By including a backdrop image---also referred to as a \"banner\" or
\"cover\" image---that captures your persona and line of work, you may
make your profile stand out.

Include your location and industry in the title. For this, you have a
character limit of 120.

Include a brief description of your background, the scope of expertise,
and the value proposition. There are 2,000 characters available for you
to use, but you don\'t have to. To make your profile stand out, you can
post or link to six examples of your work.

Include your present employment and outline its responsibilities. Be
precise. Even if you are an \"excellent communicator,\" avoid saying
anything like that. Give specifics about your communication abilities
instead, along with instances where you\'ve used them. You have 2,000
characters again.

Include at least four skills or areas of experience, as well as
information about your schooling and previous employment.

Advice: Using the Profile Strength bar, you may monitor your advancement
from \"Beginner\" to \"Intermediate\" to \"All-Star\" as you fill out
more details in your profile.

2\. Make connections with other LinkedIn users.

You may network with individuals and businesses in your sector using
LinkedIn. This is a fantastic method to remain current on
[]{#part0007.xhtml#page_16}new advancements and to exchange knowledge
with experts in your industry.

Anyone can accept your offer to connect (and you can accept their
invitation to connect with them), but to utilize LinkedIn, they must
have their account.

Your relationships on LinkedIn are saved in a list called My Network.
When you connect with someone, you get access to both their profile and
any connections that are visible to the general public. Your
\"second-degree connections\" are now these. This creates extra
networking opportunities because you can then ask them to get in touch
with you directly.

Any two LinkedIn connections that have not yet connected but who would
gain from doing so can also be introduced.

Tip: Your LinkedIn search rating is influenced by the number of
connections you have. Aiming for at least 50 \"first-degree\" ties is a
good idea.

3\. Begin conversing!

Once you\'ve established connections on LinkedIn, you may communicate
with them in real-time using the Messaging feature. Look for the green
dot next to your connections\' profile images to see who is online
according to the Active Status feature.

Additionally, the \"What time?\" and \"Great, thanks!\" contextual
shortcuts that are automatically generated by the Smart Replies feature
can keep your conversations moving along quickly.

Additionally, you can use InMail if you have a Premium account. This
enables you to communicate privately with any LinkedIn
[]{#part0007.xhtml#page_17}member without having to introduce yourself
or provide any further contact details.

4\. Offer and Accept Endorsements and Recommendations

Your connections can \"endorse\" your skills and make
\"recommendations\" for your profile, and you can do the same in return.

Personal endorsements that highlight your professional qualities are
called recommendations. Asking coworkers to write one for you will help
you amass a few of these (between five and ten is a reasonable \"rule of
thumb\").

Instead of making general statements such as, \"Bella was fun to work
with,\" ask them to specifically mention the qualities or
accomplishments that have pleased them.

Endorsements are only alerted that you possess a certain competence. If
a connection recommends you for something---leadership skills, for
instance---it may not have the same impact as specially written
recommendations, but it can still make you stand out from the
competition.

5\. Use LinkedIn Groups

To share ideas and industry news, groups can be created or joined by any
LinkedIn member. You can grow your professional network in this way.

To find intriguing groups to join, use the search box at the top of your
profile page. You may also locate new groups by looking through the ones
you\'ve previously joined. As soon as you join a group, you can
participate in discussions, post questions, and send messages to other
group members.

LinkedIn communities may be a great resource for knowledge, inspiration,
and support. People in your groups will probably reciprocate in kind if
you share your knowledge with them.

LinkedIn advises against using groups for self-promotion. Expertise
demonstration is beneficial; overt promotion of your business\'s goods
is not. If you violate a group\'s rules or etiquette, you risk being
blocked or expelled.

6\. Produce Interesting Content Particularly for LinkedIn

You can share postings from other users and post brief text updates,
photographs, and links to other websites, much like on other social
networks. But make sure that your post is pertinent to your professional
contacts and valuable, educational, and useful. You might also wish to
work on your writing!

Additionally, you can use your device to record or submit videos
directly to LinkedIn. You can do this to strengthen the brand
identification of your business or to share your knowledge and
experiences. Brighten up your video with the help of the built-in
filters and \"stickers\" and include captions for those who prefer to
watch without sound.

Through its Publishing Platform, LinkedIn also enables you to publish
whole articles. Your profile includes the articles you\'ve written.
However, you still hold the rights to any original content that you
publish, even though other users may share them and they might even show
up in search engine results. This is a fantastic approach to
demonstrating your industry knowledge.

Another alternative for distributing top-notch content is SlideShare. It
allows you to post presentations and infographics and is integrated into
LinkedIn.

Advice: Including hashtags in your postings will increase the number of
people who see them. In the search bar, enter a hashtag to discover how
many people follow it and the types of material they are most interested
in.

7\. Discover new employment opportunities.

20,000 companies in the United States are using LinkedIn to hire new
employees, according to the company\'s statistics. You can use the
service to look for individuals who have the particular knowledge and
expertise you require for your team. Depending on your sector and the
position you need to fill, it may even completely replace conventional
recruiting advertising.

You can conduct a search using a variety of different criteria using the
Advanced Search function, including location, firm, past company,
industry, language, nonprofit interests, schools, and degrees of
connection.

You have even more possibilities with LinkedIn\'s paid Recruiter plans,
which may also help you find, organize, and manage candidates. Pipeline
Builder enables you to automatically contact potential hires when they
visit LinkedIn, and the Find Nearby feature focuses on local contacts.

LinkedIn can also be used to hunt for employment. Many individuals
browse job listings as well as utilize them as a research tools.
Consider that you have received two employment offers, for instance. You
can use LinkedIn to get to know your potential new supervisors and
coworkers better as well as to get a sense of the corporate cultures of
the companies.

You may find out who has looked at your profile in the previous 90 days
and search even more effectively for job openings that fit your
preferences and skill set with a Premium Career account.

8\. Raise the Profile of Your Organization

LinkedIn may improve the exposure of your organization, your brand, and
your goods. It can also describe your business\'s history. If you do it
well, you may establish meaningful emotional bonds with both your staff
and prospective consumers.

Consider creating a LinkedIn company page so that customers, clients,
suppliers, and potential employees can learn more about your business.
LinkedIn analytics can be used by the page administrator to learn more
about the visitors to the page. This can assist you in more effectively
targeting your content.

In addition, LinkedIn has further advantages for your business. The
website can be used, for instance, to study possible partners, new
suppliers, and your competition. Additionally, the paid service, Sales
Navigator, enables you to contact potential customers and monitor
significant personnel changes in your sector.

You may combine LinkedIn with other services and platforms; for example,
Twitter can be automatically updated with your LinkedIn updates. So,
think about how LinkedIn usage fits into your company\'s broader social
media strategy.

9\. Adhere to LinkedIn\'s professional etiquette

Building and upholding a solid reputation is essential for success on
LinkedIn. This means that it\'s crucial to always present a professional
image. Therefore, refrain from revealing the kind of private information
that you might have on Facebook.
[]{#part0007.xhtml#page_21}Additionally, keep in mind that you may never
be sure who will read rumors or gossip.

Be cautious when you request or make introductions, and only do so if
you think it will be advantageous for both parties. Be careful what you
say about your company at all times.

Finally, keep in mind that the best course of action is honesty; avoid
exaggerating your accomplishments, and don\'t embellish the truth.

# WHY LINKEDIN IS THE BEST TO BUILD YOUR BRAND {#part0007.xhtml#aK1 .heading_s1Z}

In the economic world of today, it\'s not just about what you
know---also it\'s about who you know and who knows you. This used to be
a reference to the old boys\' network\'s closed shop. Thankfully, that
isn\'t the case anymore---at least mostly---anymore. The internet, or
more specifically social network sites like LinkedIn, is one of the main
causes behind this.

LinkedIn is a professional networking site used by members to forge
business relationships, locate employment opportunities, and interact
with others in their industry. This sets LinkedIn apart from other
social media platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and others.

It is a similar global success story to Facebook and Twitter. When it
first debuted in the United States back in 2003, LinkedIn focused on the
business community and was built on free membership, with additional
paid-for premium services. This helped it carve out a place in the
social networking industry. If growth started slowly, it wasn\'t for
very long. It provided []{#part0007.xhtml#page_22}services that let
businesses advertise job openings and look for potential employees on
the network.

It started turning a profit in 2007 and was launched on the stock market
four years later. It was acquired by Microsoft in 2016 for about \$26
billion (£18.5 billion) and has experienced rapid growth ever since. It
is now the go-to platform for job seekers and businesses looking to hire
new talent in nearly every field and industry you can imagine, from
graphic design to pharmaceutical manufacturing.

LinkedIn has more than 830 million members in 200 countries, including
58 million firms, and 50 million people use it to look for work every
week, according to its own most recent figures. But why is it the finest
platform for developing an employer brand and what is the story behind
these startling numbers?

First and foremost, from a commercial perspective, it enables you to
promote your organization by uploading news, videos, and images. It\'s a
method of showing the rest of the world, including potential recruits,
your values, and what you have to offer. When used wisely, it may be
used to effectively communicate your message to an audience that is
interested in what you have to say.

It takes time to develop your corporate brand on LinkedIn. It\'s not
something you construct only to leave unattended. To inform people about
your accomplishments, fresh projects you\'re working on, or intriguing
career chances, you need to post frequently. The top LinkedIn company
sites are interesting, thought-provoking, and visually appealing. They
can provide information on the office culture and working environment at
your company.

The importance of a person\'s own LinkedIn page cannot be overstated.
Twenty years ago, you would still give business cards to potential
customers and contacts, but today, everyone has an online presence that
may reveal a lot about who they are. Our LinkedIn profile frequently
serves as a person\'s first impression of us, and the distinction
between a strong profile and a passable one can determine whether or not
you are invited for a job interview.

Additionally, those looking for new chances can utilize keywords and
places to filter the job notification alerts they receive. You can
advertise that you are \"available to work,\" which serves as a helpful
signal to recruiters.

In addition to being excellent at bringing professionals together,
LinkedIn goes a step further by providing a platform for marketing your
business to a worldwide audience, boosting online sales, and developing
the abilities of your employees through online learning.

Another important element is the broad breadth of business-related
material that is published on LinkedIn. Videos fit in well with
algorithms that are designed to keep everyone\'s feeds filled with a
variety of interesting content. User-generated content (UGC) includes
blogs and photos that can be a little more personal. Long-form content
and status updates include news about your company or industry trends.

Whether users of LinkedIn should post about personal matters is
currently a topic of discussion. Some believe that platforms like
Facebook and Instagram should handle this sort of thing, but others
believe that it\'s a terrific way to develop more personal relationships
with the people in your network. Since all businesses are about people,
I support the latter since []{#part0007.xhtml#page_24}anything that
fosters more empathy and understanding must be advantageous.

Employee advocacy, which encourages individuals to post good information
on their own social media profiles, is another potent tool that
businesses are using more and more. Even though it\'s a relatively
recent tactic, it\'s popular because it works. You can connect to a
larger audience by enlisting the support of staff members who are active
on social media and have a sizable following to promote your brand. On
LinkedIn, this means you\'re reaching a business-focused audience.

The issue of authenticity is another. A post from an employee\'s account
has a higher chance of connecting with readers and earning their trust
than one from a company\'s official account. Because of this, more
businesses are using social media-savvy team members to increase sales,
attract new talent, and raise brand awareness. Additionally, this can
have a positive and negative effect on a person by simultaneously
expanding their professional network and making them feel like a vital
component of their company.

As you can see, LinkedIn is a very significant medium for building a
brand. There\'s a reason so many hiring managers and recruiters use it
in addition to the fact that it is the largest business-oriented
networking platform targeted specifically at professionals.

You can grow your company and attract top personnel by developing your
employer brand on LinkedIn. In other words, everyone benefits.

# HOW TO BUILD YOUR BRAND ON SOCIAL MEDIA {.heading_s4M}

You can create an online persona that represents your values and
abilities by sharing information online. Even if you only infrequently
use social media, anything you post, share, or respond to contributes to
this public narrative. Nowadays, your online behavior is equally as
significant as your offline behavior, particularly when it comes to your
work in digital marketing.

It takes effort to develop your brand on social media (just consider how
much work it takes to become famous on Instagram!). But if you do it
well, you might get your next job or make some useful contacts. Discover
five measures that will help you make sure your internet branding is
effective as you continue reading.

1\. Completely update your social media profiles.

Select the social media account(s) you want to concentrate on, and
delete any older accounts you don\'t use anymore. Make sure all of your
data is exact and complete for the networks you\'ll be using. You will
be able to direct and increase traffic to the networks that will feature
you and your work thanks to this.

Additionally, it can eliminate any \"questionable\" information from
earlier years that might be interpreted as having a risqué brand tone
and doesn\'t enhance your professional image.

2\. Identify Your Field of Specialization

Everyone\'s an expert at something, whether it is how to produce and
share quality content or having a comprehensive understanding of your
preferred TV program.

Is it time for you to try a few more things? Consider the content
you\'ve produced that has received the most support from your audience.
Is it possible to duplicate this with other content that is comparable
or to repurpose anything to re-engage? Your followers will begin to view
you as a leader in your chosen field the more distinctive and
interesting content you produce on it.

3\. Use Social Media Apps to Make Posting Simple

Time-consuming factors include forgotten passwords, a demanding day job,
creating content, and keeping up an online presence. However, there are
a lot of social media apps available to make life simpler.

1\. Post original content (hint: don't just dump links!)

LinkedIn marketing.

For example, many brands will break up or consolidate blog content into
smaller, text-only LinkedIn posts. Others will create a slideshow or
infographic as an alternative to an external link.

For brand accounts, we don't typically see multiple posts per day (or
